The error occurs because when you override a method, you cannot reduce the visibility of the method. The getSalary method in the
 Employee class is public, so the overridden method in the Marketers class must also be public.

Similarly, when you define a new method like message in the Marketers class, it should ideally be public if you want it to be 
accessible from outside the class, such as in the Main class.
